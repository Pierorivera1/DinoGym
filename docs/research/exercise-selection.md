# Research: exercise selection for the starter programs

Raw research notes, 2026-11-16, gathered by a research sub-agent. They feed [../TRAINING.md](../TRAINING.md). Nothing here is decided until TRAINING.md says so.

**Verification level:** Nippard's exercise tiers are second-hand. They come from write-ups of his free YouTube ranking videos on BarBend and FitnessVolt; the videos were not watched. No content from his paid programs is used. The rep ranges are our choice, based on [S17] and [HOME].

## Calisthenics candidates

**Progression rule** (from [RR]): stay on a level until every set reaches the top of the rep range with clean form. Then move up one level and restart at the bottom of the range.

Ladders read easier → **working level** → harder.

| Routine | Ladder | Sets × reps | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|

## Caveats

- No Nippard calisthenics video was found. [HOME] is his only confirmed bodyweight source.
- Most bodyweight exercises rank B or C in his gym rankings. Say so honestly in the app.
- Tiers for the pull-up, triceps exercises, hip abduction, hamstrings and calves are unconfirmed or not ranked.
